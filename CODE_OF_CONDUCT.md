# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the **PS4 Game Dump Research Database** a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient with other contributors.
- Share accurate, honest test results—both successes and failures are welcome.
- Clearly distinguish firsthand observations from unverified community reports.
- Give proper credit and direct links to external researchers, developers, and documentation authors.
- Respect privacy and legal boundaries (no personal data, no piracy, no copyrighted game files).

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Posting pirated software, copyrighted game dumps/packages, or links to unauthorized downloads.
- Fabricating test results or misrepresenting unverified claims as proven console tests.
- Publishing others' private information (personal details, console identifiers, credentials, or private repositories).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove issues, pull requests, or comments that violate this Code of Conduct or the repository's legal/privacy boundaries. Concerns can be reported by opening a private contact with the repository maintainer on GitHub.

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) principles.*
